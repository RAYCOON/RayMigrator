# Contributing to RayMigrator

Thank you for your interest in improving RayMigrator.

---

## Important

By submitting contributions (code, ideas, documentation):

- You grant RAYCOON.com GmbH the unrestricted right to use, modify, and distribute your contribution
- You do NOT gain ownership rights
- Contributions may be relicensed at the discretion of the Licensor

---

## Process

1. Fork the repository
2. Create a feature branch
3. Submit a Pull Request
4. Wait for review

---

## Guidelines

- Keep changes focused
- Follow existing coding style
- Avoid adding unnecessary dependencies

---

## Note

Contributions are voluntary and not compensated.
